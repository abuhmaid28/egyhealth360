## Status meanings

- **APPROVED FOUNDATION:** explicitly accepted IA/product meaning or binding clinical/security policy. This does not approve a new translation.
- **RECOMMENDATION:** routine proposed wording/rule, subject to content review; no implementation authorization.
- **OWNER DECISION REQUIRED:** material bundled vocabulary choice; explicit owner acceptance needed before dependent final content/design work.
- **FUTURE:** vocabulary constrained by accepted future policy; no current delivered feature or execution grant. Future translations remain proposals.

## Approval and next gate

Owner can accept CD01–CD07 together or record selected alternatives/revisions by ID after independent review. Routine strings inherit those choices and semantic tests; the owner is not asked to approve hundreds of sentences. Until acceptance, all new wording is proposed, including glossary rows marked RECOMMENDATION. Approval of a concept never approves an unsupported capability. A future entry's status remains FUTURE even after wording acceptance.

Independent content review and explicit Product Owner acceptance plus the whole-project health checkpoint precede separately authorized Brand Research. Visual Directions follow Brand Research; exactly three distinct directions require owner choice under the [charter](FOUNDATION_CHARTER.md#future-productdesign-approval-process--document-only). No Brand Research, visual design, logo/fonts/colors, Design System, prototypes, frontend/backend/runtime or deployment work starts here.
