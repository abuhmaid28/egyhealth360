## 2. Common source authority and invariants

A source belongs to exactly one patient, I2 consultation and its same-patient encounter, treating clinician/author and originating clinic, with the actual assignment, clinic membership and actor/time captured as provenance. IDs and roles come from server-resolved current identity and the consultation, never caller-selected identities. For every clinician write require a valid current session, active clinician/profile, current ACTIVE and unended assignment for that patient, the consultation's exact treating author, and the current active clinic, exact compatible active/unended membership and I2 consultation affiliation eligibility. Other clinicians with their own current assignment may read allowed signed/released material, not author or correct this source. ADMIN is operational only; no clinical payload, authoring, signing, fulfillment, impersonation, break-glass or arbitrary assignment creation. Patient own access and staff access are separate and never grant clinician authority.

For a CLOSED consultation, the same treating author may sign an existing draft and cancel, replace or privacy-restrict their source with all the current checks above, just as I2 permits source signing and correction after close. Close itself never signs, routes or releases. New independent drafts/orders are created only while OPEN; after close a replacement must be explicitly linked to the existing signed source and is limited to correcting/replacing that source. Source correction is not a general record editor. No cross-patient move: restrict the wrong-patient lineage and independently create correct-patient material only under that patient's own authorized consultation.

## 3. Medication and prescription source

A small deterministic curated medication catalog has stable opaque identity, human display name and active/publication state; bounded display metadata may distinguish entries, but no clinical recommendation, dose selection, interactions, formulary, substitution, inventory or rules engine. A newly signed item must reference a currently active published entry. Deactivation blocks new signing, never rewrites prior identity or display snapshot. Catalog maintenance is bounded operational configuration without clinical authority.

A prescription root is DRAFT → SIGNED → CANCELLED or REPLACED; terminal signed-source transitions preserve the signed snapshot. A draft is visible/editable only to its current treating author and conveys no patient or pharmacy entitlement. Draft edits require expected version; at signing validate at least one item and freeze all items, author, patient, encounter, clinic, server signing time and explicit validity. Signed prescriptions and their cancellation/replacement history are visible to the patient and currently assigned clinicians subject to privacy; drafts remain hidden. The patient cannot edit a signed source. Authorship without current authority never grants access.

Each item has stable item identity, catalog identity and display snapshot, positive exact decimal quantity, bounded nonempty unit and clinician-entered bounded instructions; no inferred dose, refill, partial quantity or substitution. Bounds, numeric precision and input validation must be explicit in Slice 1 API/schema and reject invalid values without truncation. Validity is clinician-entered at prescription level: required `validFrom` and `validUntil` instants with explicit offset, `validFrom < validUntil`; eligibility is `validFrom <= database time < validUntil`. Signing may precede `validFrom`, but `validUntil` must be future at signing. No invented medical duration, automatic extension or renewal. Validity expiry is derived, not a history transition. A signed item is NOT_DISPENSED or FULLY_DISPENSED; state is derived from a unique committed dispense fact, independently of source cancellation/expiry.

Cancellation requires a bounded nonempty human reason, actor and server time; it blocks future dispensing immediately, retaining signed items, routes and any actual dispense facts. Replacement is a single atomic reasoned command by the same currently authorized source author: create and sign a new same-patient source with new identities and explicitly entered validity/items, link old and new, mark old REPLACED, retain actor/time/reason and all prior dispensing. Replacement never silently transfers route, dispense facts, validity or entitlement. The clinician must reconcile displayed prior dispensing before submitting; the system makes no clinical decision on what to prescribe. Failure leaves old source and its entitlement unchanged. Cancellation and replacement may occur after fulfillment for truthful correction, but cannot erase it. Privacy restriction is independent of clinical cancellation/replacement.

## 4. Pharmacy route and full-item dispensing

A separate explicit clinician route command selects an active PHARMACY organization for one signed, uncancelled, unreplaced, unexpired prescription and its specified undisposed items. The same current source-author authority applies. Routing before `validFrom` is allowed; dispensing waits for validity. A route identifies exact source/items and destination, actor/time and history; it is not a patient-record grant or automatic fulfillment. At most one active destination per item. Route creation never changes signed content. Pre-acceptance reroute is an explicit atomic withdrawal and new route under current author authority, recording both; failure preserves the old route. Staff explicitly ACCEPT a route before dispensing; acceptance is operational, not a clinical fact. Once accepted, no silent transfer: reasoned cancellation/replacement and fresh source/routing are required to change destination. A route can cover multiple items; each item has independent fulfillment. Withdrawal/cancellation/replacement/expiry removes future eligibility without deleting route history. No fresh route is created by a replacement.

Staff can see/work on only their exact routed artifact and bounded fulfillment history when current session and active PHARMACY_STAFF user, active PHARMACY organization, exact active/unended membership and current artifact authority all hold. Before acceptance this is limited to the exact pending route; after acceptance it remains limited to that route. A current privacy restriction suppresses it. Completed/cancelled/expired route access is limited to its bounded correction/audit purpose under current organization, membership, artifact and privacy checks, not general browsing or new dispensing. Ending the prescriber's assignment alone does not revoke a valid route (D36). Organization suspension, membership end or session/user revocation blocks subsequent access immediately once effective, not already committed facts.

DISPENSE is an explicit atomic full-item fact after acceptance, while the route/source and validity are current. Record exact prescribed quantity/unit, medication identity, staff actor, organization, membership and database time; no client assertion of another quantity, substitute, refill or partial accumulation. One unique dispense per item across all routes; a concurrent loser receives a privacy-safe conflict after checks, exact replay receives its retained safe outcome. An erroneous fact is never deleted: authorized current staff of that exact route may append a reasoned correction/reversal with actor/time, preserving the original; reversal records that the fact was erroneous, never silently restores dispensing entitlement or permits a second fact. A fresh, explicitly signed and routed source is required for further dispensing. Show prior actual facts and correction status to authorized readers.

## 5. Laboratory orders and results

Lab is independent of pharmacy. A small deterministic test catalog provides stable identity, display name and active state; no clinical decision engine. An order has the same patient/consultation/encounter, clinician author and current authority rules as prescription. DRAFT is author-only; sign freezes test identity/display snapshot and designated active LAB organization, actor/time and source. A signed order routes to that exact lab in the same transaction; the lab explicitly ACCEPTS it, providing meaningful progress SIGNED/ROUTED → ACCEPTED → RESULT_FINAL. The clinician may reasonedly CANCEL a routed or accepted order; the lab may reasonedly REJECT a routed or accepted order. Both are terminal for new work, preserve actor/time and history, and never delete an already finalized result. Pre-acceptance rerouting is a reasoned atomic route withdrawal/reissue to a current active lab. After acceptance, change destination by reasoned cancellation and a fresh order, not silent reassignment. No lab requirement depends on pharmacy fulfillment.

Lab staff entitlement is exact order/route plus current active LAB_STAFF user, valid session, active LAB organization, exact active/unended compatible membership and valid artifact authority; no general patient browsing. Pending route access enables acceptance/rejection; accepted route enables draft and finalization; completed/cancelled/rejected artifacts allow only bounded source-correction/audit work subject to current authority and privacy. Ending the ordering clinician relationship does not cancel an otherwise valid routed order. No automatic rerouting after organization suspension.

For each order, one result root has DRAFT → FINAL; authorized staff at the routed lab enter/edit the draft with expected version and explicitly verify/finalize it. The same staff member may enter and finalize; record actual entry/editor and verifier independently with database times. Finalization requires accepted and not cancelled/rejected order, current authority and nonempty bounded human-entered result; it atomically records verification and release. DRAFT and its IDs, counts and references are invisible to patient and general clinician reads. No mandatory second verifier, doctor-release queue, sample workflow or inference.

A final result is immutable and immediately visible to the patient and any clinician with their own current assignment, subject to privacy, even if the source lab is later suspended. Authorized current staff of the exact route may append a reasoned AMENDMENT as a new verified final version or a reasoned RETRACTION as a terminal history event; each identifies predecessor, real actor/time and reason. No silent overwrite, no automatic reactivation after retraction; a fresh order is needed for new fulfillment. Amendment shows the effective corrected final clearly labelled, and permitted prior final history labelled superseded. Retraction shows a retracted status and permitted prior final history clearly labelled not current; it is never presented as a current result. A cancellation after final preserves that final and its visibility. Correction/retraction authority is limited to this artifact and survives cancellation only for bounded correction, with current staff/org/membership and privacy checks.

## 6. Privacy, replay and transactions

Wrong-patient restriction is an independent, append-only, reasoned exact-source action by the current treating source author with current clinical and originating-clinic authority, including after close. Restrict the whole affected root/lineage and linked route, dispense or result projections; never move records between patients. Until restriction, appropriate author, patient, clinician and exact recipient access apply. Once restricted, suppress payload, root/version/item IDs, lineage links, counts, embedded references and notifications from all ordinary patient, clinician and staff projections; no forensic endpoint or existence-revealing conflict. A corrected patient gets an independently authorized source. The restriction command returns only a safe acknowledgement on exact replay. Existing source restrictions do not automatically propagate across unrelated modules; each affected I3 root must be restricted explicitly, and I3 must not copy restricted I2 content. No notification subsystem is introduced.

Every mutation, including catalog/config changes, drafts, edits, sign, cancel, replacement, routing, acceptance, dispense, corrections, order rejection, result finalization and privacy restriction, requires canonical UUIDv4 operation key scoped by actor and command. Canonical validated payload fingerprint includes exact target, expected version, item data, reason and destination where applicable; server-owned actor/time are not client-controlled. Commit operation record, safe retained result reference, state/history and minimized audit together. Lock/authorize current actor, requested target and any prior operation's target and check both privacy scopes **before** revealing replay or payload conflict. Exact replay returns the retained outcome projected under current authority and current privacy; it cannot duplicate facts, history or audit or revive entitlement. If current authority or privacy no longer permits disclosure, deny uniformly even for exact replay. Changed payload with same key conflicts only after those checks. A rolled-back transaction consumes no key. A timeout with uncertain commit requires the client to explicitly retry the identical command/key after reauthorization; no automatic clinical mutation retries.

Generic audit is append-only, atomically committed, and limited to actor, action, opaque entity, server time and safe operational provenance. Do not put instructions, clinical narratives, medication details, lab values, reasons or secrets in generic audit. Reason and clinical payload belong only in access-controlled source history. Read/list filtering must happen before count and pagination; restricted/nonexistent/unauthorized target and conflict responses must not distinguish existence. No notification may expose restricted content or references.

## 7. PostgreSQL concurrency contract

Use one transaction, bounded lock/statement/transaction timeouts, server clock, deterministic lock ordering and rechecks after waits and immediately before commit. Coordinate with **the existing revocation writers**, not merely a new private lock: lock user/session, patient/profile and current assignment, organization/membership and relevant affiliation in an order compatible with I2 and I1; lock consultation/source, route and item/result in stable identity order. Shared/exclusive row or advisory locks must conflict with actual assignment end, session revoke, org suspension, membership end, privacy and competing I3 writers; document and test the concrete lock graph before implementation. Lock absence/uniqueness races via a stable parent or advisory lock plus database unique constraints; use conditional version/state updates. Recheck effective eligibility and `clock_timestamp()` after lock waits. If revocation commits first, the operation fails; if clinical mutation commits first, revocation blocks subsequent operations. Expiry/session expiry at final check denies. No claim of recalling a delivered response.

Real PostgreSQL two-connection tests with barriers must prove both commit orders for: sign/sign; assignment end/sign; session revoke/sign; clinic membership end/sign; cancel/dispense; replace/dispense; dispense/dispense; pharmacy org suspension or membership end/dispense; finalize/finalize; finalize/cancel; finalize/correction or retraction; lab org suspension or membership end/finalize; privacy restriction versus read, replay and mutation. One committed full dispense per item and one finalization per result version; losing commands never append duplicate facts. Race outcomes must correspond to a valid serial order; cancellation or replacement first prevents later fulfillment, fulfillment first is preserved. A session or validity expiring while waiting must deny. Audit insert failure, forced rollback, lock/statement timeout, deadlock and connection failure leave no partial clinical fact, history, audit or consumed key; a client may safely retry same key only after resolving uncertain commit and current checks. Never silently retry a deadlock, timeout or clinical mutation.

## 8. Persistence and bounded API

Future migrations are additive only: preserve every historical migration byte/checksum and existing populated data, FKs, guards and behavior. Use same-patient composite constraints, state/version checks, immutable signed/version records, unique operation identity and one-dispense-per-item constraints, with database-owned times and atomic audit. New tables/concepts are catalog entries; prescription roots/items/signed snapshots and transitions; route/item bindings and dispense facts/corrections; test catalog, lab order/route/transitions, result versions; exact privacy restrictions and operation records. Exact physical layout is implementation work. No fabricated backfill of prescriptions, routes, orders, dispenses or results; absence remains absence. Migration proof includes empty and populated baseline upgrades, historical checksum and row preservation, repeat deploy and rollback of failed transaction.

The following are **logical route groups**, not current HTTP endpoints. Every mutation uses Section 6 replay and privacy ordering; list/detail reads recheck current authority, filter before counts, and use bounded deterministic pagination. All clinical target denial is uniform for unauthorized, absent and restricted records. Transport naming may follow existing `/api/v1` conventions without changing these semantics.

| Resource / operations | Actor and authority | Visible state / mutation |
|---|---|---|
| Medication/test catalog list; bounded catalog maintenance | Authenticated clinical author for list; current ADMIN for operational maintenance only | Published active identities for selection; maintenance affects publication, never clinical payload; keyed mutation. |
| Consultation prescription list/detail, create/edit draft, sign, cancel, replace, restrict | Exact treating source author with current assignment and originating clinic checks | Author sees own unrestricted draft and signed history; writes as Sections 2–3; keyed/versioned. |
| Patient own and assigned clinician prescription list/detail | Patient own, or current ACTIVE assignment | Signed source, allowed route/dispense history and clear cancellation/replacement/correction; no drafts or restricted references. Read only. |
| Prescription route/withdraw/reroute | Current exact source author | Signed eligible items and destination; explicit keyed transition and preserved history; no transfer after acceptance. |
| Pharmacy queue/detail, accept, dispense, correct/reverse | Current exact routed pharmacy staff and active org/membership | Only exact routed artifact and bounded fulfillment history; pending/accepted/completed scope as Section 4; keyed mutations. No patient record browse. |
| Consultation lab order list/detail, create/edit draft, sign/route, cancel, pre-acceptance reroute, restrict | Exact treating source author with current assignment and clinic checks | Author's draft and signed history; keyed/versioned transitions. |
| Lab queue/detail, accept/reject, draft/edit, verify/finalize, amend/retract | Current exact routed lab staff and active org/membership | Only exact order and result needed for work/correction; keyed/versioned mutations and verified provenance. |
| Patient own and assigned clinician order/result list/detail | Patient own, or current ACTIVE assignment | Signed order and FINAL result with explicit amendment/retraction history; no drafts, draft references/counts or restricted lineage. Read only. |

Patient/clinician reads are independently authorized and unaffected by recipient-org suspension for already visible sources. No staff, ADMIN, patient or unrelated clinician gains mutation authority from IDs. API responses must never call prescribed/dispensed data confirmed actual use; separately reported actual use is outside this sprint.

## 9. Implementation slices and evidence

Each slice requires separate authorization, bounded vertical API/persistence and proof. Dependencies permit pharmacy and lab to be developed independently after shared source foundations; no giant combined PR.

1. **Medication catalog + prescription source lifecycle** — first recommended slice. Implement curated catalog and draft/edit/sign/cancel/atomic replacement/restriction, patient/current-clinician reads, source and item history, same-patient provenance, authority/replay/privacy and additive migrations. Item fulfillment initially always NOT_DISPENSED; no route/dispense, lab, reported-use or UI claims. Replacement records prior dispensing correctly once Slice 2 exists. Required routes are catalog, author source commands and safe patient/clinician reads above. Require unit/input/API tests, authorization matrix including closed consultation and revoked author, privacy/count/replay tests, concurrent sign/sign and assignment/session/membership revocation races, failure/rollback/audit tests, populated migration/checksum proof and exact-head CI. Exit evidence: working end-to-end authorized signed source, preserved reasoned cancellation/replacement and history, no access leakage, explicit verification of all listed cases, and independent review. This recommendation is **not authorization**.
2. **Pharmacy routing + full-item dispensing** — exact route/accept/reroute, current D36 checks, one full-item fact, correction and source integration. Evidence: two-connection dispense/cancel/replace/dispense and suspension/membership races, privacy, replay and rollback; real source-to-dispense journey and migration proof.
3. **Lab order source + routing** — bounded catalog, clinician source and lab route, acceptance/rejection/cancellation and independent exact authority. Evidence: actual author-to-designated-lab journey, cross-org/role denial, current revocation, history, privacy, replay and migration tests.
4. **Lab result draft/final/release + corrections** — draft entry, explicit verification/finalization, immediate patient/current clinician visibility and reasoned amendment/retraction. Evidence: same-person entry/verify, draft invisibility, correction/retraction and source suspension, finalize races, current authority, privacy, audit failure/replay and migration proof.
5. **Combined I3 journey, security and exit review** — exercise sources, independent lab/pharmacy fulfillment and visibility together without inferring adherence or action completion. Evidence: real PostgreSQL races in Section 7, populated upgrade/checksums, security/IDOR/privacy and failure paths, end-to-end API/runtime and required CI, independent exact-head review. No imaging or I4/I5/EACCE.
