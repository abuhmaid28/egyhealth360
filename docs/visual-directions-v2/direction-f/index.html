F — Next-Gen Health OS

Exploratory proposals. No selected direction, logo, palette or typeface. Accepted UX, IA and content remain the foundation.

All three directionsEquivalent-content comparisonAll six logos
Focused, connected, digitally native. Graphite horizontal workspace; a luminous appointment band; flat divided clinical panes; cyan used only for orientation and action. Typography study: IBM Plex Sans + Noto Sans Arabic. A dark workspace can feel technical and needs real-device glare testing. “OS” is a review label, never a claim of complete health coverage.

F1 · Routed context

Three stepped bands align around a shared axis, showing a handoff between independent sources with no infinite-loop or AI symbolism.

A stepped digital silhouette; possible network/fast-forward reading. It must not suggest automated care or guaranteed speed.

F2 · Distinct endpoints

Two angular frames face a narrow shared channel. Each source stays legible while responsibility moves between them.

Separate framed endpoints; test resemblance to architecture or hardware. Does not represent unrestricted data sharing.
