PR #79 visual directions are NOT accepted as the product visual direction.

PRODUCT OWNER DECISION: REJECT A / B / C AS CURRENT FINAL-DIRECTION CANDIDATES.

This is not a rejection of the Foundation Reboot work or its UX/content foundations. It is specifically a rejection of the current visual quality and aesthetic direction.

Product Owner feedback is authoritative:

- Direction A is strongly rejected.
- Do not reuse the journal, paper, editorial, casebook, parchment, notebook, magazine, or document-like visual metaphor.
- Direction B is closer to a real modern application and has some useful structural thinking, but its visual execution is still substantially below the required quality bar.
- Direction C also feels closer to a modern real product, but its current purple/brown palette is rejected and its overall execution is still substantially below the desired professional/premium level.
- B1 and C1 contain some conceptual potential, but neither is close to an approved logo. They may be explored, radically refined, or discarded.
- No existing logo concept is selected.
- No current palette, font family, visual direction, or UI treatment is approved.

NEW QUALITY BAR

EgyHealth 360 must look like a premium, current-generation digital product when opened on both web and mobile.

The target is not:
- “good for a graduation project”
- “clean enough”
- “a healthcare dashboard”
- “technically modern”
- “three color variations of one layout”

The target is:
- premium
- highly polished
- unmistakably modern/current-generation
- digitally native
- visually confident
- professionally art-directed
- elegant without being decorative
- information-rich without clutter
- excellent Arabic and English typography
- consumer-quality Patient experience
- professional high-density Clinician experience
- credible enough to resemble a funded production product

REFERENCE PRINCIPLES — DO NOT COPY PRODUCTS

Study the product-design quality of excellent modern digital products such as:
- Linear
- Vercel
- Stripe
- Apple Health
- One Medical
- other world-class contemporary healthcare and SaaS products

Extract principles, not layouts, logos, proprietary assets, or branding.

Important qualities to study:
- calm density
- strategic minimalism
- excellent type hierarchy
- refined grids
- disciplined spacing
- sophisticated information hierarchy
- restrained color
- purposeful surfaces
- excellent navigation
- high-quality states
- subtle depth
- purposeful micro-interactions
- responsive/mobile-native composition
- visual identity that remains recognizable without excessive decoration

STRICT VISUAL REJECTIONS

Do not create:
- journal UI
- paper UI
- editorial-document metaphors
- beige/parchment themes
- the current purple Direction C palette
- generic healthcare blue templates
- excessive cards
- giant rounded cards everywhere
- random gradients
- excessive glassmorphism
- oversized empty whitespace that reduces information usefulness
- generic Bootstrap/admin-dashboard styling
- generic shadcn-default appearance
- cartoon healthcare illustrations
- medical crosses/hearts/capsules used as lazy branding
- futuristic sci-fi interfaces
- decorative elements with no product purpose

TYPOGRAPHY IS A PRIMARY DESIGN REQUIREMENT

Do not treat typography as a later token choice.

Explore high-quality typographic systems for:
- English
- Egyptian Arabic / Modern Arabic UI
- mixed Arabic + Latin medical content
- numbers
- dates
- medication names
- measurements
- dense clinician interfaces

Arabic must feel intentionally designed, not merely RTL-enabled.

MOBILE IS A FIRST-CLASS PRODUCT

Do not create desktop screenshots and simply stack them for mobile.

The Patient mobile experience in particular must feel like a premium native-quality health application.

Clinician mobile surfaces must be purpose-built for appropriate mobile tasks rather than squeezing the full desktop workstation onto a phone.

COLOR DIRECTION

Do not freeze a palette yet.

Explore sophisticated, modern palettes using disciplined combinations such as:
- graphite
- slate
- deep navy
- refined neutral whites/grays
- high-quality blue / cyan / teal accents where appropriate

Color must have purpose.

Avoid the rejected A parchment palette and rejected C purple/brown identity.

LOGO EXPLORATION

Restart logo quality exploration.

B1 and C1 may be retained only as conceptual seeds.

Generate genuinely stronger logo concepts with:
- distinctive silhouette
- strong 16px / 24px / 32px recognition
- excellent app-icon potential
- excellent monochrome behavior
- modern healthcare credibility
- no generic hospital symbols
- no cliché heart/cross/capsule/AI sparkle
- no fake “360 completeness” claim
- a concept connected to care continuity, coordination, responsibility, trusted handoff, or understandable healthcare
