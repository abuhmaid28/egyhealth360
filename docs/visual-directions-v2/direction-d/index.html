Measured, precise, quietly capable. Calendar-led patient orientation; neutral vertical navigation; fine rules and compact parallel clinical panes. Typography study: Inter + Noto Sans Arabic. A disciplined grid can feel impersonal. Test warmth through readable language and patient hierarchy rather than extra decoration.

D1 · Aligned custody

Two offset, interlocking steps meet across a protected negative-space channel. A deliberate transfer, without a closed-circle completeness claim.

Geometric handoff; could resemble a logistics mark. Compare against financial and collaboration identities before selection.

D2 · Accountable edge

A single clipped tile has an open internal edge and a separate endpoint: responsibility has an identifiable boundary and owner.

Compact tile silhouette; test for file/storage interpretation. It is a brand concept, not a document metaphor for the UI.
