The shared lesson is craft through hierarchy, type, spacing, useful density, accurate states and purpose. No single reference decides the palette or layout. The requested quality is a premium current digital product; the owner decides whether these proposals reach it. Reference quality cannot be transferred through superficial resemblance.

## Applied art-direction constraints

- D uses measured alignment, fine source rows and a calendar-first appointment. It has no paper texture, notebook, journal or editorial metaphor.
- E uses a single purposeful appointment anchor and open content. Human language and letterform rhythm replace decorative health illustration.
- F uses graphite work planes and precise accent placement. It avoids science-fiction effects, glass, neon, random gradients and simulated AI.
- Shared safety colors are independent of identity and always have text; an accent never means medical clearance.
- Typography is authored together across scripts, including full RTL screens, long names, diacritics, mixed medical quantities, dates, labels and small marks.
