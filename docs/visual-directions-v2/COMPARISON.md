## Navigation and interaction studies

Patient primary destinations remain My care / متابعتي and Find care / دور على دكتور. Appointments, prescriptions, shared summaries/follow-up, available history and doctors are explicit secondary retrieval. Clinician primary destinations remain Schedule / جدول المواعيد and People in your care / مرضاي. Documentation, prescription, follow-up and review/close are local to a consultation, not new global modules. No Pharmacy/Lab/Admin rearchitecture is introduced.

Mobile Doctor work is deliberately bounded to selecting and reviewing an authorized visit. It does not squeeze desktop prescribing or signing controls onto a phone. Both languages preserve exact patient/reference/clinic/date/time/authority. The specimens do not implement real actions.

Micro-interaction proposal: 140ms color feedback and 1px pressed movement for explicit controls, visible focus, no animated clinical success and no simulated mutation. `prefers-reduced-motion` removes that transition. Loading is stable and language-preserving; uncertainty calls for exact-source verification and never automatic re-signing. All seven state types are shown in each direction and language.
