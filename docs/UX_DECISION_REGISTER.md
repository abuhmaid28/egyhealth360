## Status meanings

- **APPROVED FOUNDATION:** already approved by existing owner policy/accepted contracts or this sprint's explicit boundary. It does **not** mean the agent has approved a new product choice.
- **RECOMMENDATION:** proposed Stage 2 Product UX guidance, including candidates sufficiently grounded for owner acceptance. Not a finalized journey, task ranking, navigation or implementation grant.
- **OWNER DECISION REQUIRED:** material choice to resolve through review before dependent work. No need to block preparation of this complete reviewable package.
- **FUTURE:** later phase/delivery, no authorization or completion implied. Existing accepted future policy remains binding when that work is authorized.

## Approved foundation candidates for owner review

**Already frozen by prior approval, not newly approved here:** five groups; current/future/Live distinctions; current assignment and exact fulfillment authority; independent release/close; full-item-only supply and immutable correction; follow-up versus booking; Admin operational limits; current privacy and explicit recovery. UXD01–UXD09 cite their prior authority.

**Sufficiently grounded candidates to accept next:** behavioral role goals; P0/P1 emphasis; Patient next-step clarity; Clinician consultation context; Pharmacy assigned-work model; future Lab coverage within accepted policy; complete-task continuity, the ten principles, hierarchy and anti-pattern requirements. UXD10–UXD16 remain RECOMMENDATION until the Product Owner accepts/revises them. No agent-approved final journey or screen is hidden behind “foundation.”

## Recommendations and assumptions

Routine recommendations do not require a questionnaire: task frequencies are qualitative; avoid repeated explanations/vanity counts; make exact status/responsibility understandable; progressively disclose permitted detail; keep optional fulfillment branches independent; evaluate complete tasks in both languages. No fixed screen count, content density, navigation pattern, SLA, notification, preferred clinical workflow or clinical rule is inferred.

Lab work-status grouping, frequency estimates and future source-linked continuity presentation remain hypotheses. Existing accepted order/result/correction policy is not reopened by that uncertainty. Exact pharmacy “ready for pickup” language cannot be selected because there is no readiness/stock fact; “accepted” must not be relabeled to imply it.

## Owner decisions required next

Only three material decisions are raised, UXD18–UXD20: accept/revise this Product UX package; choose later integration emphasis/sequence where the proposed experience exceeds V1 exposure; and authorize Stage 3 after independent review plus whole-project health disposition. These are review questions, not requests to approve tiny wording or implementation choices. Until then this PR remains open/unmerged with auto-merge off.
