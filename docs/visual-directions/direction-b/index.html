Two independent logo ideas

B1 / Aligned handoff

Three staggered bars align into a stable exchange: different responsibilities share an order, not unrestricted information.

Similarity / cliché risk: Generic infrastructure/logistics overlap. Avoid interpreting bars as clinical measurements.

B2 / Focused frame

An open rectangular boundary holds one exact focal item. The frame is separate from the handoff-bar concept.

Similarity / cliché risk: Scanning/security-tool association is possible; no monitoring or guaranteed protection claim. Central detail is secondary at 16px.

No trademark search or legal clearance claimed. Both marks remain unselected. A1/B1/C1 in screen headers are placement specimens only.
