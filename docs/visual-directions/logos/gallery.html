B1 — Aligned handoff. Three staggered bars align into a stable exchange: different responsibilities share an order, not unrestricted information.

B2 — Focused frame. An open rectangular boundary holds one exact focal item. The frame is separate from the handoff-bar concept.

C2 — Folded path. A broad folded route keeps its turn visible and leaves its end open. The solid angular silhouette differs from the open orbit.
