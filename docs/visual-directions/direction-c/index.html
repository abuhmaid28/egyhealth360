Concept sheet

Core idea
A wayfinding system: distinct stations of responsibility connected by a visible, bounded thread.
Visual personality
Know where care stands.
Logo family
C1 Open interval + C2 Folded path — equal exploration candidates
Shape language
Broad round ends, vertical spines and open station markers
Surface philosophy
Quiet lavender canvas with a continuous white care lane; color is concentrated in orientation
Information density
Progressive Patient stations; Doctor task spine with an adjacent state ledger
Color philosophy
Indigo wayfinding over lavender neutrals; warm brown source annotations. A visible station is not a completed clinical step.
Typography philosophy
Trebuchet MS / system sans + Noto Kufi Arabic / Noto Sans Arabic fallbacks — exploratory only; no final family.
Icon / illustration philosophy
Route/station glyphs that describe navigation, never clinical progress or automated inference
Patient experience feel
Guided, connected, oriented
Doctor experience feel
A navigable workspace with visible independent source states
Arabic fit
Kufi display anchors with readable sans body; logical RTL station order
Strengths
Strong orientation and recognizable movement between related tasks.
Weaknesses
The thread adds visual weight and may suggest a mandatory sequence unless labels stay explicit.
Risks
A circle could overpromise completeness; station markers can resemble a progress tracker. No global completion state.
What makes this distinct
Horizontal masthead, rounded care lane, station spine, Kufi display and indigo wayfinding.

Two independent logo ideas

C1 / Open interval

An interrupted orbit meets a short independent handoff. The opening deliberately avoids an all-care-complete loop.

Similarity / cliché risk: Circular/360 category crowding; could imply completeness if paired with careless copy. No arrows or emergency emblem.

C2 / Folded path

A broad folded route keeps its turn visible and leaves its end open. The solid angular silhouette differs from the open orbit.

Similarity / cliché risk: Transport/wayfinding overlap and an abstract P reading; not an ECG or pharmacy capsule. Test independent recall.

No trademark search or legal clearance claimed. Both marks remain unselected. A1/B1/C1 in screen headers are placement specimens only.
