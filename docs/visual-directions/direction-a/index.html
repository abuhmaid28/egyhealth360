Care Journal

No winner selected. All logo, palette and typography choices are exploratory. Owner outcomes: A / B / C / REJECT / HYBRID.

Concept sheet

Core idea
An attentive editorial journal: a clear next responsibility, followed by source-labelled pages of care.
Visual personality
Care, kept legible.
Logo family
A1 Open folio + A2 Attentive pair — equal exploration candidates
Shape language
Open margins, fine rules and gently softened paper edges
Surface philosophy
A warm continuous page; only the next responsibility receives a tinted inset
Information density
Generous patient reading; a two-column clinical casebook with a stable identity margin
Color philosophy
Forest identity on warm paper; clay marks editorial attention. Functional states keep their independent meanings.
Typography philosophy
Georgia + Noto Naskh Arabic / Noto Sans Arabic fallbacks — exploratory only; no final family.
Icon / illustration philosophy
Small open-line symbols with full labels; no decorative medical illustrations
Patient experience feel
Personal, unhurried, readable
Doctor experience feel
A considered casebook with deliberate writing and review
Arabic fit
Naskh-led reading rhythm; generous line height and sentence-led hierarchy
Strengths
Calm reading and a clear distinction between the next action and source retrieval.
Weaknesses
Long Arabic passages increase scrolling; the paper metaphor could feel less immediately digital.
Risks
Could drift toward premium editorial styling or paper bureaucracy. Dense tables need careful restraint.
What makes this distinct
Serif/Naskh display, warm paper, open editorial columns and margin annotations. No permanent sidebar for Patient.

Two independent logo ideas

A1 / Open folio

Two unequal leaves hold a readable place in care; the open spine signals a bounded source, not a complete chart.

Similarity / cliché risk: Editorial/publishing overlap; the page silhouette needs recognition testing. No medical-emblem or emergency reading observed.

A2 / Attentive pair

Two counter-facing human gestures carry attention between people. This is a relationship idea rather than a folio variation.

Similarity / cliché risk: Social/conversation reading is possible; it must not promise a messaging service. At 16px the open body detail is less distinct.

No trademark search or legal clearance claimed. Both marks remain unselected. A1/B1/C1 in screen headers are placement specimens only.
