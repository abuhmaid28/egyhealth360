## Personality and intended experience

Retain the accepted BR01–BR08 research strategy: calm, attentive, precise, human and locally fluent. The flowing form supplies warmth; dark teal and disciplined type supply credibility; gold is a restrained identity accent. Premium means clear hierarchy, care in spacing and useful restraint. Avoid decorative clinical dashboards, magical AI cues, theatrical gloss everywhere or claims that everything is medically fine.

Patients should feel respected, oriented and able to understand their next supported action. Clinicians should see a credible, efficient working environment. Pharmacy and Laboratory need clear responsibility and source states; Admin needs operational clarity. All five workspaces share this identity without new role-specific logos or implied clinical authority. Laboratory capability remains subject to its separate product authorization.

## Foundation usage principles

Use the supplied variants rather than rebuilding the logo with CSS or substituting emoji/icons. Keep the symbol's gradients in primary uses; use the supplied monochrome derivative where one ink is required. Allocate clear space, use calm backgrounds and reduce brand prominence before shrinking important care content. Do not encode clinical success, warning, dispensing or permission solely with brand turquoise/gold.

Detailed proposals: [palette and measured contrast](PALETTE.md), [typography](TYPOGRAPHY.md), [logo usage and minimum sizes](LOGO_USAGE.md), [NOVA/header application](HEADER_GUIDANCE.md). These are recommendations for review, not runtime tokens or authorization to replace existing accepted UI.

## Misuse examples — prohibited applications

| Example | Why it fails / correct application |
| --- | --- |
| Stretch the mark to fill a short navigation slot | Distorts the selected silhouette. Use uniform scaling and a compact placement. |
| Crop the floating circle or close the cavity | Removes defining human-care geometry. Preserve the entire symbol bounds and clear space. |
| Recolor the ribbon as a clinical warning or recolor marks per workspace | Confuses identity and clinical state. Keep the selected asset; use independently defined state labels. |
| Put the transparent master over a busy photo or a similar turquoise patch | Loses edges and the negative-space reading. Use a quiet compatible surface or approved supplied background variant. |
| Shrink the full bilingual/tagline lockup into a 16px favicon or narrow mobile bar | Text becomes unreadable. Use the existing standalone/app/favicon asset and separately legible product text. |
| Add another heart, cross, AI sparkle or an infinity shortcut | Changes the chosen identity. Do not add symbols or restart exploration. |
| Treat the current Arabic tagline or collided lettering as a frozen final export | Hides known polish debt. Label current reference artwork and retain DP01/DP02 until owner disposition. |
