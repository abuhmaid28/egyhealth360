## Material difference requirement

Each direction must explain its concept and evidence, then show a coherent alternative across all dimensions below. Use equivalent tasks and comparable content so review is fair. Three palettes on the same interface, three logos on one generic SaaS shell, or different marketing moodboards without product examples fail.

| Dimension | What each direction must demonstrate later | Difference without policy change |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Personality expression | Its way of being credible, human and locally fluent | Distinct expression of accepted strategy, not contradictory clinical promises |
| Logo concept family | At least two materially different candidates with rationale | Symbol logic, silhouette and wordmark relationship differ; not recolors |
| Shape language | How grouping, boundaries and rhythm support tasks | No default rounded-card system copied across all three |
| Surface philosophy | How content, separation and emphasis work | Coherent alternative treatment with readable status/actions |
| Information density | Patient orientation and populated Doctor work | Different hierarchy/density philosophy while preserving safety context and accepted IA |
| Color philosophy | Role of identity, emphasis, neutrals and semantics | Distinct reasoning and usable functional states, not hue substitution |
| Typographic personality | Arabic/English optical relationship and hierarchy | Distinct expressive approach with exact medical content preserved |
| Icons / illustration | Functional and optional expressive roles | Distinct meaningful approach; a no-illustration approach may be valid if reasoned |
| Patient feeling | Existing care, next step and personal relevance | Do not replace My care with a sales/search homepage |
| Doctor feeling | Credible orientation and efficient clinical attention | Do not replace consultation with metrics or a decorative concept screen |

All directions must preserve accepted primary meanings: Patient **My care / متابعتي** + **Find care / دور على دكتور**; Doctor **Schedule / جدول المواعيد** + **People in your care / مرضاي**. Secondary appointment/prescription/summary/follow-up/bounded-history retrieval stays explicit and discoverable. Consultation remains sustained local work, not separate global clinical modules. Visual alternatives do not reopen IA or accepted terminology.

**Minimum total: 6 logo concepts.** Six tiny variations of one icon do not qualify. Changes only to hue, stroke, rotation, corner radius or placement do not establish material conceptual difference. Neither an algorithmic score nor a designer's preferred candidate can become the final logo. Ahmed must see all candidates before any logo is finalized.

Each candidate must have a rationale, strategic connection, intended recognition, cliché/similarity risks and tradeoffs. Show its English-name relationship, use in an Arabic product context, small app-icon/header context, standalone silhouette and monochrome behavior. Explain full **EgyHealth 360** versus compact-space treatment without silently deleting 360 or implying complete medical coverage. If an Arabic lockup requires a new transliteration, surface a separate explicit owner choice; do not invent it.

Mandatory evaluation criteria: distinctive, memorable, simple at small sizes, legible, scalable, clear silhouette, credible in healthcare, not childish, not a stock clinic icon, viable as app icon and beside the English name, coherent with Arabic experience, monochrome-capable, independent of gradients/tiny details. Assess cliché and similarity risk. No unsupported medical function, AI diagnosis, emergency service, hospital/government ownership or pharmacy-only reading. Protected emblems/confusing imitations and copied competitor marks are excluded. Similarity screening is preliminary; **do not claim legal trademark clearance**.

## Semantic and anti-clone constraints

- Connected care never means shared access to every record. Patients see own permitted sources; current clinical assignment governs Doctor reads/writes; booking, role, membership and authorship alone grant no clinical authority. Scope selection changes presentation, never permission.
- Private notes remain private; summary and follow-up sharing are explicit independent acts; prescription signing is not dispensing; closing does not save/sign/share other material. No single visual “done” indicator may imply otherwise.
- Pharmacy acceptance is responsibility, not stock, readiness or recorded supply; recorded dispense is not consumption; reversal retains history and never renews entitlement. Admin has operational scope and no blanket clinical access or override.
- Future Lab remains FUTURE; no current result, specimen workflow, mandatory doctor-release queue or second-verifier requirement. Integrated I4 history, reminders, AI/EACCE and production medical readiness are not present claims.
- Preserve honest Demo/connected/unsupported states. No optimistic clinical success, automatic mutation retry, hidden-record count or named denial that leaks existence.
- No copied competitor logo, layout, whole palette, distinctive illustration or protected asset; no near-clone of Vezeeta/MyChart/any reference. Learn patterns and record what differs. Do not download competitor brand assets into the repository.
- Egyptian relevance does not automatically require pyramids, pharaonic symbols, Eye of Horus, flag colors, Nile, lotus or hieroglyphics. None is preapproved. Any motif needs a reason and explicit review; language/usability comes first.
- Avoid card soup, unrelated metrics, generic blue-gradient cards, random blobs, meaningless art, excessive glass, neon AI theatre, decorative sparkles, giant marketing figures in clinical work, excessive chips, copied fintech styling and irrelevant medical icons. Functional exceptions need rationale, not a mechanical universal ban on containers.
