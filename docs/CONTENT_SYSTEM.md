## Voice and tone

Clear, calm, competent, human, concise, respectful, non-alarming and clinically honest. Prefer one fact per sentence; use familiar concrete nouns, active verbs and an explicit object. Avoid hype (“seamless care”), robotic (“artifact processed”), legalistic prose, overly cheerful clinical feedback, blame and unsupported reassurance (“everything is fine”). “No data” is not “no condition”; “saved” is not “shared.”

Message priority: what happened → current state → available action → what happens next → reference detail only when needed. Put exact identity, source and consequence alongside a consequential action. Do not repeat “Patient” throughout the personal app; doctor-side patient/visit identity must remain explicit. Name a person as recorded; avoid guessed gender, titles or credentials. English instructions use “you”; Arabic avoids unnecessary pronouns and gender-specific honorifics.

| Audience | Tone and example English | Authored Arabic equivalent |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Patient | Simple and actionable: “Your doctor recommended follow-up. No appointment is booked from this recommendation.” | أوصى طبيبك بمتابعة. هذه التوصية لا تحجز موعدًا. |
| Doctor | Compact, clinically explicit: “Private note signed. The patient cannot view it.” | وُقّعت الملاحظة الخاصة. لا يمكن للمريض الاطّلاع عليها. |
| Pharmacy / future Lab | Exact responsibility: “Pharmacy acceptance recorded. No medicine has been recorded as dispensed for this item.” | سُجّل قبول الصيدلية للوصفة. لم يُسجّل صرف الدواء لهذا البند. |
| Admin | Scope/effect: “Organization suspended. Clinical history is retained.” | عُلّقت الجهة. يبقى السجل السريري محفوظًا. |

No urgency/triage advice, diagnostic interpretation, medicine recommendation or timing/notification promise is generated by content. Authored clinical instructions are not automatically rewritten, translated, abbreviated or replaced with generic advice.

## Status language system

Status is a source-specific meaning, not an enum prettified into Title Case. Show object and relevant actor; never flatten booking, consultation, document, prescription, route, per-item dispense and correction into one global “Complete” state. Rows with derived meaning are explicitly not new backend states. Roles: P patient; D doctor with current source authority; F exact pharmacy staff; A bounded Admin; L future exact Lab staff. Unauthorized/hidden sources have no visible status or existence/count hint.

## Action language system

Use verb + exact object. Name consequence in button/review text; avoid Submit, Confirm, Done or Continue for consequential mutations. Arabic buttons use concise verbal nouns consistently (حجز الموعد), explanatory instructions may use imperatives (راجع الموعد). Do not concatenate a generic verb and translated object; author the full phrase with agreement. Accessible action names include the selected object/date/person where needed, with permitted identity only. No icon-only meaning or vague “click here.”

## Content accessibility and length guidance

Keep simple complete sentences, explicit field labels, and named action targets. State essential status and consequence in words; color, icons, punctuation, position and animation cannot carry meaning alone. Expand abbreviations on first use when they are needed; do not abbreviate medication/unit content arbitrarily. Avoid vague links, decorative text in accessible names and repeated role words that obscure identity. Error identifies the safe next step, not just “invalid.” Preserve screen-reader order of medicine, quantity, unit, time and actor. Arabic diacritics may disambiguate verbs such as سُجّل, not decorate all text.

| Type | English guidance | Arabic guidance | Safety override |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Navigation | Usually 1–3 words | Usually 1–4 words, natural possessives | Do not hide appointment/prescription/history retrieval to meet length |
| Button | Usually 2–5 words | Usually 2–6 words | Exact action/object/consequence beats compactness |
| Field label | Usually 1–5 words | Usually 1–6 words | Quantity/unit/validity distinctions explicit |
| Helper | 1–2 short sentences | 1–2 independently authored sentences | Explain unknowns and boundaries; no literal fragment assembly |
| Status | Usually 1–5 words plus necessary qualifier | Usually 2–7 words plus needed agreement | Split state axes; retain actor/object |
| Success | Usually one sentence | Usually one sentence | Confirm only actual committed state |
| Error/uncertainty | Usually 2–3 short sentences | As many short sentences as safe recovery needs | Never truncate identity, reasoned limitation or duplicate-prevention guidance |

These are editorial guidance, not pixel or enforced character limits. Arabic may naturally be longer. No layout, fonts, colors, component styling, Design System, visual accessibility implementation or frontend code is selected.
