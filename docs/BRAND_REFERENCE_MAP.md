## Method and evidence strength

Purposive sample: **13 real products/services** spanning Egyptian healthcare/pharmacy (3), regional public healthcare (1), global patient healthcare (3), clinical software (2), wellness (1), AI clinical software (1), Egyptian non-health services (1), and bilingual regional services (1). These are overlapping comparison needs, not a market-share ranking. Five additional public guidance references support functional requirements. No inspiration-gallery concepts are market evidence.

**FACT** means a directly verifiable published fact, including the fact that a supplier makes a claim; it does not independently verify that claim. **OBSERVATION** means visible public-page content or presentation. **INTERPRETATION** means our explanation of its relevance. **RECOMMENDATION** is a proposed action for EgyHealth, not owner acceptance. Market prevalence, user trust and preference were not measured.

Text pages were opened through web research. Separate browser inspection used public desktop pages at 1365×950, without login or transactions. Nine meaningful rendered pages support visual observations: Chefaa, Yodawy, MyChart, Accurx, Careem, NHS, Headspace, Heidi and DrChrono. InstaPay rendered its header/brand treatment but not its main hero; Doctolib rendered a blank surface; Vezeeta's browser request reached a challenge. Their text evidence remains usable, but unobserved appearance is not inferred. Sehhaty was text-only. Temporary browser captures stayed outside the repository and are not deliverables or copied brand assets. No authenticated clinical workflow or native app was tested.

## Product references

Every entry below was accessed **2026-11-02**. Each has an observation, relevance interpretation and explicit avoid-copy boundary.

### R01 — Vezeeta: Egyptian discovery/booking platform

Source: [Arabic Egypt entry](https://www.vezeeta.com/ar). **OBSERVATION, text:** doctor/specialty/location search leads the page, with adjacent consultation, pharmacy and offer content. Familiar Egyptian phrasing coexists with medical nouns. Browser visual inspection was blocked; no palette/logo conclusion is made here.

**INTERPRETATION:** finding care should be understandable immediately, but commercial acquisition is a different purpose from returning to existing care. **RECOMMENDATION:** preserve Find care under accepted IA, while My care carries existing-care orientation. **Do not copy:** search-page layout, ranking/superlative claims, promotions, assets or service breadth; none establishes EgyHealth's clinical suitability, pharmacy delivery or market standing.

### R02 — Chefaa: Egyptian digital pharmacy

Source: [Arabic about/services](https://chefaa.com/eg-ar/about-us). **OBSERVATION, text + visual:** family photography, a green-accented Arabic identity on white, conversational reassurance and separate prescription, delivery and medication-reminder descriptions.

**INTERPRETATION:** recognizable people and natural language can express care without ancient-Egypt motifs. Photography alone cannot demonstrate privacy or service reliability. **RECOMMENDATION:** explore respectful human warmth and task-specific explanation. **Do not copy:** its family image, identity, promotional strip, green treatment or delivery/refill/reminder promises. EgyHealth records bounded fulfillment; it does not offer these services.

### R03 — Yodawy Care Program: Egyptian recurring medication service

Source: [Care Program landing page](https://careprogram.yodawy.com/). **OBSERVATION, text + visual:** an Arabic monthly-medication proposition, blue/cyan actions, dark headings, family photography, an orange promotional badge and explicit service assurances.

**INTERPRETATION:** a narrow service promise is easier to articulate than an undifferentiated feature catalogue. **RECOMMENDATION:** explain the exact care handoff and next responsibility. **Do not copy:** partner marks, page composition, discount language, coverage, medication licensing or delivery assurances. Supplier statements here are not verified operating facts for EgyHealth.

### R04 — Sehhaty: regional public healthcare platform

Source: [Saudi Ministry of Health service description](https://www.moh.gov.sa/en/eservices/sehhaty/pages/default.aspx). **FACT, publisher description:** the Ministry describes a unified health-services platform including appointments and access to health information. **OBSERVATION, text-only:** government identity/verification surrounds the service explanation.

**INTERPRETATION:** one platform can connect different care activities, but institutional authority comes from a real mandate. **RECOMMENDATION:** communicate coherent services with precise boundaries. **Do not copy:** government emblems, institutional assurance, national-health-record implications, eligibility or service catalogue. No Sehhaty app visual assessment was performed.

### R05 — MyChart: global patient portal

Source: [MyChart public site](https://www.mychart.org/l/en-us/). **OBSERVATION, text + visual:** multiple patient tasks are explained together; the public page pairs a colorful wordmark, playful object illustrations, light surfaces and blue actions.

**INTERPRETATION:** healthcare identity can accommodate warmth while covering more than booking. This marketing page does not prove usability of a hospital's configured portal. **RECOMMENDATION:** test broad care meaning with source-specific retrieval. **Do not copy:** wordmark, object illustrations, tiles or organization-dependent record/sharing/family capabilities. EgyHealth must not imply a complete record or imported proxy authority.

### R06 — Doctolib: global patient discovery/service

Source: [French patient entry](https://www.doctolib.fr/). **OBSERVATION, text:** specialty/name/place search precedes explanations of care access and privacy. Browser rendering was blank; extracted text, not the blank image, supports this entry.

**INTERPRETATION:** public discovery and ongoing service can coexist without making every function a primary destination. **RECOMMENDATION:** distinguish acquisition from ongoing care. **Do not copy:** its search composition, identity or messaging/reminder/prevention promises. No clinician-console or authenticated patient experience was reviewed.

### R07 — NHS App: global public patient service

Source: [NHS App introduction](https://www.nhs.uk/nhs-app/). **OBSERVATION, text + visual:** a blue institutional header, restrained neutral background, explicit setup/help routes and clear linked headings; public eligibility is stated.

**INTERPRETATION:** support and legible service structure can carry confidence without ornamental medicine symbols. **RECOMMENDATION:** make supported next steps and limitations obvious. **Do not copy:** NHS identity, institutional authority, typography, exact layouts or England-specific service/eligibility rules. NHS trust cannot be transferred through a similar blue.

### R08 — Accurx: clinician/patient communication software

Source: [Patient explanation](https://www.accurx.com/patient). **OBSERVATION, text + visual:** teal identity, dark text, generous white space and a heart-shaped photo composition; provider-mediated communication is explained alongside published assurance claims.

**INTERPRETATION:** warmth and explicit responsibility can coexist. A familiar heart is not distinctive merely because it signals care. **RECOMMENDATION:** pair humane expression with specific audience/source boundaries. **Do not copy:** heart/photo construction, mark, claims of NHS assurance, encryption certification or communication channels. Those are not EgyHealth evidence.

### R09 — DrChrono: clinical EHR software

Source: [EHR product page](https://www.drchrono.com/ehr/). **OBSERVATION, text + visual:** charting/scheduling and other practice tasks are described in one proposition; blue/pale surfaces, clinician photography and small workflow illustrations frame the marketing page.

**INTERPRETATION:** clinical seriousness needs task vocabulary and recognizable work, not an enterprise appearance alone. **RECOMMENDATION:** validate Schedule and consultation under realistic density. **Do not copy:** chart layout, identity, billing/practice-management scope, AI or integrated-platform claims. This is marketing evidence, not a hands-on EHR review.

### R10 — Headspace: wellness/mental-health service boundary reference

Source: [Public consumer entry](https://www.headspace.com/). **OBSERVATION, text + visual:** warm yellow/orange accents, playful illustrated characters, rounded sections and differentiated therapy/meditation offers.

**INTERPRETATION:** playfulness can make a service approachable, but its suitability is task-dependent. **RECOMMENDATION:** evaluate warmth without importing wellness metaphors into prescribing or clinical warnings. **Do not copy:** characters, orange identity, animations, layout or therapeutic/AI-companion capabilities. No assertion that playful healthcare is universally ineffective is supported.

### R11 — Heidi: AI clinical software boundary reference

Source: [Heidi public entry](https://www.heidihealth.com/). **OBSERVATION, text + visual:** explicit AI positioning, prompt-like interaction imagery, serif display type, warm pale surfaces and yellow/dark accents; the page does not support treating all AI brands as neon-gradient clones.

**INTERPRETATION:** category confusion can come from function cues as much as color. **RECOMMENDATION:** avoid assistant/prompt/automated-treatment framing for EgyHealth. **Do not copy:** brand, flower-like mark, hero, typography combination or AI care-partner functions. Published AI claims are not validated here and grant no EgyHealth AI capability.

### R12 — InstaPay: Egyptian non-health transactional service

Sources: [English](https://www.instapay.eg/?lang=en), [Arabic](https://www.instapay.eg/?lang=ar). **OBSERVATION, text + partial visual:** parallel service/how-to explanations, recipient verification and transaction-status topics; the rendered Arabic header uses a purple identity with an orange accent. The main hero did not render, so no complete visual-layout conclusion is supported.

**INTERPRETATION:** exact target and outcome explanations are useful analogies for consequential care actions. “Trusted” here names the comparison need, not a measured trust score. **RECOMMENDATION:** test understandable confirmed versus uncertain outcomes. **Do not copy:** purple/orange branding, transfer receipts, speed claims or financial transaction/retry semantics; clinical commands retain their own contracts.

### R13 — Careem: regional bilingual service ecosystem

Sources: [English UAE](https://www.careem.com/en-AE/), [Arabic UAE](https://www.careem.com/ar-AE/). **OBSERVATION, text + Arabic visual:** an Arabic wordmark, green identity, strongly grouped service areas and a prominent campaign; corresponding English task groups are available. Some mixed-language content remains visible.

**INTERPRETATION:** a coherent localized service brand can extend across activities; literal string parity does not ensure equal experience. **RECOMMENDATION:** test both languages in complete tasks. **Do not copy:** wordmark/green system, service tiles or campaign styling. This UAE reference does not establish availability of every service in Egypt, Egyptian dialect fitness or accessibility quality.

## Cross-reference conclusions

1. **OBSERVATION:** the inspected pages vary in warmth, surface treatment and category cues. Blue, green and white recur, but warm and multicolor approaches also exist. This sample cannot establish market-wide frequency.
2. **INTERPRETATION:** recognizable identity plus specific service behavior is a stronger research proposition than “a trustworthy color.” Trust still needs operational proof.
3. **RECOMMENDATION:** learn from clear responsibilities, local language and source-specific feedback. Keep competitor expression and unsupported service promises out of EgyHealth.
4. **Evidence gap:** no Egyptian Patient/Doctor interviews, recall experiment, color preference study, authenticated competitor review or trademark search was conducted. Later hypotheses and tests are in the [exploration brief](BRAND_EXPLORATION_BRIEF.md).
