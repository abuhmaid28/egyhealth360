## Status meanings

- **INHERITED FOUNDATION:** prior explicit acceptance/contract, not a new agent decision or new implementation grant.
- **RECOMMENDATION:** proposed IA choice, available for independent review and owner selection; not final.
- **OWNER DECISION REQUIRED:** material bundled choice requiring explicit acceptance before dependent work; the proposal can be completed without an interim questionnaire.
- **FUTURE:** later scope/delivery; no active Live capability or present execution authorization. Accepted future policy remains binding.

## Owner review gate

The five material gates IA01–IA05 are collected in the [owner decision package](INFORMATION_ARCHITECTURE.md#9-owner-decision-package-and-deferred-naming). A decision should identify selected candidate/IDs and revisions, then separately authorize any next stage after independent review and the whole-project health checkpoint. Merging documentation or passing CI cannot silently change these statuses. No new material navigation choice is marked owner-approved here.

Working-label decisions are naming **requirements**, not approved strings: distinguish existing care from finding care; pending relationship from active grant; recommendation from booked appointment; signed source from released summary; acceptance from actual supply; corrected/reversed from renewed entitlement; final result from clinician review; bounded history from comprehensive record. All exact Arabic/English terminology and date/time/number conventions remain deferred.
