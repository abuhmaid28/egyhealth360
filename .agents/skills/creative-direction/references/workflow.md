1. **Brief.** Pin issue authority, sole writer, HEAD/tree and routes. Inventory authorized fields, user goals, behavior and state sources. Research existing user journeys and lawful public references. Record observations and relevance without copying visual assets or inspecting patient records. Separate observed gaps from hypotheses.
2. **Explore.** Compare three distinct compositions or task hierarchies in browser-rendered specimens when authorized. Clear the rights of any included material before capture or export. Use matching synthetic fixtures, representative Arabic/English and mobile/desktop views. Explain tradeoffs against the brief.
3. **Decide.** Record explicit Product Owner selection of the versioned visual artifact, approved scope, copy and tokens. Obtain separate source authorization and verify the [rights plan](../../asset-rights-provenance/references/asset-manifest.md) before use or export.
4. **Implement.** The authorized frontend owner receives approved artifact and source pins, exact paths, component mapping, states, bilingual copy, privacy constraints and rollback. Preserve existing roles, routes, state semantics and clinical contracts. Booking grants zero clinical authority.
5. **Prove and review.** Compare equivalent browser captures using the [evidence manifest](../../visual-review-and-a11y/references/evidence-manifest.md). Independent governance/security and visual/accessibility reviewers record findings and verdicts against the exact final tree. Track functional CI and visual acceptance separately.
6. **Resolve and accept.** Fix in-scope defects and recheck affected cases. Escalate changed design, rights, behavior or contracts. Owner visual acceptance, publication, release and deployment follow their respective gates. Keep the approved baseline available for rollback.

## Quality gates

| Gate | Evidence and failure rule |
| --- | --- |
| Functional and privacy | All critical states and routes exercised, preserving authorization, session boundaries and synthetic fixture isolation. Zero unexpected external requests, writes or clinical grants. Required regression failures block acceptance. |
| Localization and layout | Compare EN/LTR and AR/RTL, Light/Dark/System and 320, 390, 768 and 1440 CSS px. Check mixed-script names and separately verify 200% page zoom and 200% text. Zero unintended overflow, clipped content or obscured actions. |
| Accessibility | WCAG AA, including 4.5:1 for normal text, 3:1 for large text and 3:1 for applicable non-text contrast. Pilot touch targets 44×44 CSS px. Check semantic labels, keyboard and reverse traversal, focus visibility/restoration, Escape where applicable, forced colors and reduced motion. Zero unresolved AA defects. Record automated coverage and manual checks. |
| Performance | Measure three comparable loads with fixture, build, device, cache and throttle held constant. Report median LCP, CLS and transferred bytes. Proposed pilot gate for Owner ratification: LCP ≤2.5s, CLS ≤0.1, no >10% median LCP or byte regression. Record missing probes. |
| Aesthetics | Score hierarchy, composition/alignment, brand distinctiveness and healthcare restraint separately on 1–3 (deficient, acceptable, excellent). Cite actual browser evidence and rationale. A score of 1 requires correction or Owner disposition. |
