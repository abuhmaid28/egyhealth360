# Asset rights provenance

Inventory fonts, icons, imagery, marks and derivatives in the [asset manifest](references/asset-manifest.md). Verify source, rights holder, permission for each channel, trademark, license, attribution and digest. Check privacy independently. Limit use and disclosure to the documented channel in the manifest. Block unverified assets and derived evidence outside that channel. Suggest original geometry, text or initials subject to scope and approval. Preserve AP05.
